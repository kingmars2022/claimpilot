# Northbridge Software — Employee Handbook (excerpt)

*Northbridge Software is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes.*

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days in their first year of service.
From the third year of service, the annual vacation allowance increases to 20 days.
From the tenth year of service, it increases to 25 days.

Vacation requests must be submitted in PeopleHub at least 10 business days before the first day off.
Your manager approves or declines the request within 3 business days.

Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Carried-over days
must be used before March 31, after which they are lost.

## Sick days

Employees have 7 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days do not carry over.
For an absence longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical note must be sent to hr@northbridge.example.

## Hybrid work

Employees work on site at least 2 days per week. Team leads choose the on-site days for their team.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department director and HR.

## Parental leave top-up

Northbridge tops up government parental benefits to 90% of base salary for up to 20 weeks.
Employees become eligible after 6 months of continuous service.

## Who to contact

- Payroll and benefits: hr@northbridge.example
- Workplace health and safety: safety@northbridge.example
