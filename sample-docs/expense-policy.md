# Northbridge Software — Expense Reimbursement Policy

*Northbridge Software is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes.*

## General principles

Work-related expenses are reimbursed when they are reasonable, approved and supported by a receipt.
Claims are submitted in the Finance portal no later than 30 days after the date of the expense.

## Client meals

Business meals with clients are reimbursed up to $75 per person, including taxes and tip.
The claim must state the client company's name and the purpose of the meeting.
Alcoholic drinks are limited to $20 per person.

## Travel

- Mileage with a personal vehicle: $0.68 per kilometre.
- Train or bus: economy class.
- Hotel: up to $250 per night in Montreal and Toronto, $200 elsewhere in Canada.

## Approval

- Under $500: manager approval.
- From $500 to $2,000: manager and department director approval.
- Over $2,000: prior approval from Finance is required before the expense is incurred.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

Parking tickets, flight upgrades and personal expenses are not reimbursed.
