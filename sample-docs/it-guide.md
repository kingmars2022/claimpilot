# Northbridge Software — IT Support Guide

*Northbridge Software is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes.*

## Connecting to the VPN from home

1. Open the SecureLink client on your company laptop. It is pre-installed on new laptops; if you
   cannot find it, install it from the Software Center.
2. Enter the server address vpn.northbridge.example.
3. Sign in with your company account and password, then approve the multi-factor authentication
   (MFA) prompt in the Authenticator app on your phone.
4. When the connection succeeds, the SecureLink icon in the taskbar turns green.

If the connection fails three times in a row, restart your computer and try again. If it still
fails, open an IT ticket.

## Password rules

- Your company account password must be changed every 90 days.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot match any of your last 5 passwords.
- After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes.

## Lost or stolen laptop

Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT within 24 hours at it-help@northbridge.example or
extension 4357. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely.

## Requesting new software

Submit a request in the Software Center; your direct manager approves it. Software that needs a
paid licence also requires IT approval, usually within 3 business days.

## IT service desk

- Email: it-help@northbridge.example
- Extension: 4357
- Hours: Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. (Eastern Time)
